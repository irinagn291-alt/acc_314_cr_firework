The app never asks for camera, photos, microphone, location, or tracking. No permission prompt appears, and there is no usage-description string.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

CR Firework is a painting hang for people who want to spot the one work that does not belong. You save Yale University Art Gallery paintings on this device, hang four unlabeled tiles (three by one maker, one stray), and tap the stray.

No credentials or demo account are needed. Cold launch shows a full-screen splash illustration with no words, then either onboarding or the hang. On a first launch that has not finished onboarding, a three-page cover appears. A page indicator is present (VoiceOver: "Page 1 of 3", "Page 2 of 3", "Page 3 of 3"). The bottom button is always "Continue". Page 1 headline "Save a school." Line "Keep Yale paintings on this device. Home is the hang, not a museum walk." Top-trailing "Skip" (VoiceOver: "Skip onboarding") ends onboarding and opens the hang. "Continue" goes to page 2. Page 2 headline "Hang four." Line "Three share a maker. One does not. The tiles stay unlabeled." "Skip" and "Continue" work the same way. Page 3 headline "Pluck the stray." Line "A true tap saves that painting. A miss greys that tile and stays." There is no "Skip". "Continue" ends onboarding and opens the hang. After onboarding, the hang is home. On Simulator only, a first run can already mark onboarding complete, hang a live school, file isolated paintings (including "Four Jockeys"), and record at least one miss so Saved is not empty. That seed does not run on a physical device. A device first run after onboarding is the idle hang until you save a school. On the idle hang, tap "Explore". Title "Explore". Empty search or first open lists the local Yale shelf. Tap "Save" on at least three works by one maker and at least one by another. A new save shows "Saved to your hang." Tap "Close", then tap "Hang four paintings". Tap the painting that does not belong. A true tap files that painting as isolated and shows "Saved" / "The stray is saved. Hang the next school." A miss greys that tile and shows "Miss" / "That tile missed. The hang stays." Tap "Saved" to open "Isolated paintings and misses". Tap "Settings" to open "Yale credit and Retract". On Settings, "Contact" opens the Horsserie contact page, the rows "Yale University Art Gallery" / "artgallery.yale.edu" and "Yale LUX" / "lux.collections.yale.edu" each open that source, "Re-run onboarding" returns to the three onboarding pages, "Reset all data" opens "Reset all data?" with "This removes paintings, picks, and misses on this device.", destructive "Reset all data", and "Cancel", and "Retract last pick" peels the last correct pick or the last miss. "Pluck one stray" is not on the hang bar; Shortcuts "Open hang then pluck" opens it. Siri and Shortcuts can also open the app with "Open Quiz", "Open Explore", "Open Saved", "Open Settings", "Hang a school", and "Pluck the stray".

Paintings, picks, and misses stay on this device. Search talks to the Yale collection and falls back to the local shelf. On Settings, "Contact" opens the Horsserie contact page.

There is no region lock. Copy is English only. Counts follow the device number format.

No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies. The app is an Education painting hang. Paintings come from the Yale University Art Gallery.

The app does not show health, medical, or product-health information. It is a personal log only and gives no recommendations.

The app does not scan barcodes or QR codes.
